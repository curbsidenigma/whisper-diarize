Interview in English about the onboarding process for new engineers at a mid-sized software company. The participants are the interviewer and Alex Rivera (engineering manager). They discuss how new hires get access to systems, who mentors them during the first weeks, where the process breaks down, and what they would change. Vocabulary: onboarding, ramp-up, buddy, mentor, access request, provisioning, SSO, VPN, staging, production, code review, pull request, pairing, sprint, standup, retro, backlog, ticket, runbook, on-call rotation, handoff, blocker, escalation, feedback loop, 30-60-90 plan.
