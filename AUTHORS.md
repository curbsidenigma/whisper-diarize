## Built on

This project is a thin pipeline over other people's substantial work:

- [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) (MIT) and
  [CTranslate2](https://github.com/OpenNMT/CTranslate2) (MIT), by SYSTRAN
- [WhisperX](https://github.com/m-bain/whisperX) (BSD-4-Clause), by Max Bain
- [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) (MIT), by OpenAI
- [pyannote.audio](https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio) (MIT), by
  Hervé Bredin and contributors

The pyannote diarization models carry their own gated terms of use, which you
accept on Hugging Face before first use. Those terms cover the models, not this
repository.
